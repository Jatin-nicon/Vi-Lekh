# Proposal

## Why

When exporting annotated lecture notes to PDF via `pdf-lib`, freehand pen strokes and highlighters currently render as jagged chains of straight line segments rather than continuous, smooth curves. In addition, floating annotation text boxes render with an opaque white background that blocks out the underlying slide content and diagram text.

## What Changes

- **Smooth Vector Bezier Strokes in PDF Export**: Use `pdf-lib`'s native `page.drawSvgPath` with quadratic Bezier curves generated by `pointsToSvgPath(stroke.points)` and round line caps (`LineCapStyle.Round`), rendering continuous, organic pen and highlighter paths that match the on-screen SVG.
- **Translucent Highlighter Blending**: Apply `BlendMode.Multiply` and continuous Bezier paths for highlighters to eliminate overlapping butt-cap opacity spikes and knots.
- **Transparent Annotation Text Boxes**: Remove the solid white rectangle fill (`color: rgb(1, 1, 1)`) from `drawRectangle` during export, ensuring text box backgrounds are 100% transparent and underlying slide content remains completely legible.
- **Round Line Caps on Straight Underlines**: Set `lineCap: LineCapStyle.Round` on underline paths.
