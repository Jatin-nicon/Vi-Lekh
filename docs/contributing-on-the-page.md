# Suggesting changes and leaving comments

A short guide for anyone who reads a note and wants to improve it. You need to
be signed in for both.

## Suggest changes on the page

Open a note and press **Suggest edits**. The note opens as it looks in the
reader, and you edit it right there. Your changes go into a private draft that
saves as you type. Nothing changes in the published note until a course editor
reviews and merges your suggestion.

- **Change text:** click a paragraph, heading, list item, table cell or card
  text and type. Click anywhere else to keep the change. **Enter** starts a
  new line inside the same part and **Esc** cancels. Pasted text loses its
  formatting.
- **Add something:** after you click a part, a bar appears above it. Pick
  **Case Study**, **My Experience**, **Findings**, **Paragraph** or **Image**
  to add it after that part. A new section is credited to you and opens for
  typing. If you clicked a list item or a table cell, the new part goes after
  the whole list or table.
- **Undo and redo:** use the buttons, or **Ctrl+Z** and **Ctrl+Y** (or
  **⌘Z** and **⌘⇧Z** on a Mac) when you aren't typing in a part.
- **Parts you can't edit:** drawings, answer boxes and some formatted parts,
  such as a paragraph with a formula, can't be edited on the page. When you
  click one, a message offers **Leave a comment**, so you can describe the
  change for the reviewers instead.

### Before you submit

Press **Submit for review**. You first see **Review your changes**, with the
version you started from on the left and your draft on the right:

- Yellow parts changed, green parts are new and red parts were removed.
- The two sides scroll together. The arrow buttons jump from one change to
  the next.
- On a phone, switch between **Before** and **After** with the tabs.

Press **Continue to submit** to add a short summary and send it, or
**Back to editing** to keep working. **Review changes** opens the same view at
any time without submitting.

## Comments

A comment proposes an idea or asks a question without editing the note.
Only you and the course's reviewers can see the comments you start.

- **On some text:** in the Web view, select the text and press the
  **Comment** button that appears next to it.
- **On a whole part:** open **Comments** and press **Pick a block**, then
  click the part.
- Comments are up to 2,000 characters.

The **Comments** button shows how many open comments you can see. Its panel
lists them. Click a comment's quote to jump to the highlighted text on the
page, or click highlighted text to open its comment.

- **Reply:** whoever started a comment and the course's reviewers can reply.
- **Edit or delete:** you can edit or delete only your own comments and
  replies. Deleting a comment also deletes its replies.
- **Resolve:** whoever started a comment, or a reviewer, can mark it resolved
  when it's been dealt with, and reopen it later. Resolved comments are listed
  under **Resolved** and are no longer highlighted.
- If the text a comment was made on has changed since, the comment shows
  "Made on an earlier version" instead of a highlight.

### For course editors

The **Reviews** page lists **Open comments** from the courses you review,
next to suggestions waiting for review. Click one to open the note with that
comment showing.
